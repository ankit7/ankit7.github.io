Brands now have one more thing to worry/hire for - AEO/GEO professionals. As if SEO were already not nightmarish. You don't just want to rank anymore cause who has patience to visit the organic results but now you want to be cited when AI is summarizing, ahem helping.

Here is the article, optimized for AEO/GEO, covering both terms AEO/GEO. I've also added [Action items](#actions) at the end. As the terms have almost similar meanings, I will use AEO throughout.

## What is AEO?
AEO, or Answer Engine Optimization, is optimizing your content to get picked up by AI services such as Claude, ChatGPT, Google's AI Overviews, Perplexity, and others so it gets cited in their answers as a source.

## What is GEO?
GEO, or Generative Engine Optimization, is the same underlying idea as AEO but meant for AI chatbot-style services (ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini, etc.). Most teams use the two terms, AEO and GEO, interchangeably.

## Are AEO or GEO different from SEO?
Nope. AEO is a broader term (covers Snippets, People Also Ask, voice, Gen AI answers) and GEO is a subset of AEO, being more focused on popular AI chat services (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini,  etc.). Google treats AEO/GEO as ordinary SEO.

## Is AEO a separate ranking system?
No. It uses existing __ranked index__ rather than retrieving new results. So, if you are not being ranked for the original keyword, AEO won't save ya.

## Do I have to be in the first page or top 10 results for AEO?
No. Ahrefs did a study and found that only around 38% of cited results were in top 10.

| Organic ranking position | Share of AI Overview citations |
|---------------------------|-------------------------------|
| Top 10                    | 37.9%                         |
| Positions 11–100          | 31.2%                         |
| Not in top 100             | 31.0%                         |

*Source: [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-overview-citations-top-10/)*


## Does JavaScript rendering hurt AI visibility?
Not for mighty Google. But for other AI bots (GPTBot for ChatGPT, ClaudeBot, etc.) - Yes, at least for now. Server-rendered content is better suited.

| Rendering | Google Search / AI Overviews | Other AI bots (GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot) |
|---|---|---|
|Server-rendered | Fine | Fine |
|Client-rendered (JS-only) | Fine | Invisible |

## What content unit gets extracted for AEO/GEO?
The passage relevant to the query gets extracted, and not the entire page. That is the main difference from traditional SEO, where the whole page competes for a keyword. The passage has to be able to stand alone as a complete answer. Use evidence (numbers, a table, a short list) to support claims.

## Can one page supply the cited passage for several related prompts?
Yes, this is query fan-out. Google's example: "how to fix a lawn full of weeds" fans out into "best herbicides for lawns," "remove weeds without chemicals," etc. A page optimized to answer that whole cluster might earn citation on multiple prompts.

## Will the page be cited if it already appears in featured snippet and PAA boxes?
Most probably, yes. If the content was already in featured snippets or in People Also Ask (PAA) boxes, odds are very high. Google Search's AI features use similar ranking and retrieval system to those two formats.

## Does schema markup or llms.txt help?
Not confirmed. Google's May 2026 guidance says structured data isn't required for AI Overviews/AI Mode, and names llms.txt directly as one of the tactics that doesn't help.

## Should you publish on Reddit, YouTube, and LinkedIn for AEO?
Yes, but weight it to your query mix rather than publishing everywhere by default. Broad, consumer topics get cited most on YouTube and Reddit. LinkedIn, as expected, gets more cited on B2B or professional topics.

| Platform | Best for |
|---|---|
| YouTube | Broad/consumer topics |
| Reddit | Broad/consumer topics |
| LinkedIn | B2B/professional topics |

Source: [Ahrefs , June 2026, 2.96M prompts (US)](https://ahrefs.com/blog/most-cited-domains-ai-overviews/)

## How do you measure and report on AEO/GEO?
Manual scraping and a good old spreadsheet. Track the same set of questions (related to your business) over time (ideally each week for data collection and monthly for trend/reporting) and log these four things.

__Mention Rate__, __Reference Rate__, __Answer Position__ and __Tone Score__.

![AEO reporting overview](/static/images/aeo-reporting.png)

Mention Rate is how often your brand gets named at all. Reference Rate - how often it actually gets linked or cited (not just mentioned in passing). Answer Position - how high up it appears when it is cited. Tone Score tells you whether the AI talks about you in a good, neutral, or bad way.

## Action items {#actions}
1. Ensure indexation and snippet eligibility for target pages.
2. If using client-side rendering - view source on key pages to confirm main content is in the raw HTML. Prioritize server-side rendering for key content/pages.
3. Build a prompt inventory of 20–50 real questions which might be asked by your target users. Get them from PAA, site search, support tickets, and sales calls.
4. Turn page headings into those actual questions. Answer each in 40–60 words in the section's first 1–2 sentences, then follow with evidence (numbers, table, list).
5. Cover the head query's likely fan-out cluster on the page, not just the literal keyword.
6. Keep Organization/Product schema for entity clarity and rich-result eligibility only. Stop pitching FAQ schema or llms.txt internally as an AI-citation strategy.
7. YouTube (with accurate captions/transcripts) and Reddit for broad/consumer topics, LinkedIn (company + employee posts) for B2B/professional topics.
8. Log four metrics weekly, per engine, per prompt: Mention Rate, Reference Rate, Answer Position, Tone Score - don't conflate "named" with "cited".
9. Read the gaps: low Mention Rate = awareness gap. High Mention Rate with low Reference Rate = named but not trusted. Weak Answer Position or negative Tone Score = passage needs a clearer or more favorable rewrite.
10. Track each distribution channel's citation share separately from your own domain. It moves independently and fast.
11. Roll weekly logs into a monthly trend report, and re-test the same prompt set.
